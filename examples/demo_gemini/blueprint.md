# Launch Blueprint: Proteo Crisps

**Verdo Foods S.r.l.** · launch in France · generated on 2026-11-11

## 1. Executive summary

Verdo Foods S.r.l. will launch Proteo Crisps in France targeting weekly sport practitioners. This segment was selected because it represents 17.5 million adults (34% of adults) with a 41% purchase intent, providing the volume required for company goals over vegans and strict vegetarians, who show 72% purchase intent but account for only 1.3 million adults (2.5% of adults). 

Proteo Crisps are positioned as an oven-baked legume snack delivering high protein content and convenient everyday snacking without palm oil or additives. Main distribution and marketing channels are supermarkets and hypermarkets, specialised sport and organic shops, online, gyms and vending, and Instagram. The campaign bridges active training and daily snacking under the tagline "Le croustillant sain et protéiné pour vos objectifs." No blocking issue is open. The primary open gaps are the missing product price and competitor price analysis, the unallocated marketing budget, and the exact launch day in March 2026.

## 5. Strategy

**Target:** SEG2 Weekly sport practitioners. Weekly sport practitioners who practice sports weekly and represent the largest consumer group in the French market.

**Positioning.** For weekly sport practitioners, Proteo Crisps are the oven-baked legume snack that delivers high protein content and convenient everyday snacking without palm oil or additives, unlike mainstream store brands and sport-oriented bars.

**Value proposition.** High protein content for recovery and convenient everyday snacking.

**Pricing direction.** Competitor prices are unknown and a detailed price comparison has not been carried out; pricing should be decided after conducting a specific competitive price analysis against mid-level brands like Gerblé and supermarket private labels.

**Channels:** Supermarkets and hypermarkets, Specialised sport and organic shops, Online, Gyms and vending, Instagram

**Rationale.** SEG2 is chosen as the primary target because it comprises 17.5 million adults, representing 34% of adults and the largest consumer group, with 41% stated purchase intent [6]. Although SEG1 has a higher purchase intent at 72%, its estimated size is only 1.3 million adults (2.5% of adults) [6], which is too small to achieve the ambitious goal of reaching a 2% value share of the French protein snack category and 1,500 points of sale in the first year [5]. SEG2 provides the necessary volume and matches the company goals. The selected channels directly align with the market distribution breakdown where supermarkets and hypermarkets account for 68%, specialised sport and organic shops 17%, online 9%, and gyms and vending 6% [1].

## 6. Campaign

**Concept.** Proteo Crisps bridges the gap between active training and daily snacking for weekly sport practitioners. By highlighting our 15 g of protein per bag and clean recipe with no palm oil or additives, we position Proteo Crisps as the ideal oven-baked legume snack for recovery and everyday convenience.

**Tagline:** *Le croustillant sain et protéiné pour vos objectifs.*

| Channel | Headline | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Supermarkets and hypermarkets | L'encas protéiné idéal pour vos courses | Découvrez Proteo Crisps au rayon salé : des chips de légumineuses cuites au four, 15 g de protéines par sachet, sans huile de palme ni additifs. |
| Specialised sport and organic shops | La puissance des légumineuses pour votre récupération | Alliez exigence sportive et plaisir naturel. Proteo Crisps offre 15 g de protéines végétales par sachet, sans additifs et certifié bio et sans gluten. |
| Online | Commandez vos encas protéinés en ligne | Simplifiez votre quotidien sportif. Recevez vos sachets de Proteo Crisps chez vous et profitez d'un snack riche en protéines pour vos pauses et entraînements. |
| Gyms and vending | Le snack post-entraînement par excellence | Besoin d'un en-cas après l'effort ? Prenez un sachet de Proteo Crisps : 15 g de protéines et une cuisson au four pour un plaisir sain. |
| Instagram | Votre nouveau rituel sportif et gourmand | 15 g de protéines, 6 ingrédients simples, zéro compromis. Intégrez Proteo Crisps dans votre routine sportive hebdomadaire ! |

**Content ideas**

- In-store tasting stands in major French supermarkets during the launch month to let weekly sport practitioners sample Sea Salt and Rosemary flavours
- Partner displays in specialised organic and sport nutrition shops highlighting the clean label and 15 g protein content
- Targeted Instagram video ads featuring active adults enjoying Proteo Crisps after a running or gym session
- Sponsored tasting corners in partner gyms and vending machine placements for post-workout snacking
- Unboxing and snack-prep bundles shipped to fitness and wellness micro-influencers in France
- Digital banner ads on sport and wellness e-commerce platforms promoting the 9-month shelf life and compostable packaging

**Visual direction.** Clean, dynamic and natural style using earthy greens, warm terracotta tones, and crisp white typography. Imagery features vibrant action shots of weekly sport practitioners alongside minimalist product flat-lays emphasising the compostable packaging, legume ingredients, and clean 6-ingredient list.
