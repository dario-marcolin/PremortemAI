## 1. Executive summary

This document outlines the strategic launch plan for Verdo Foods’ flagship product, **Proteo Crisps**, into the French market in March 2026. Building on its profitable growth in Italy, Verdo Foods aims to scale internationally by entering the €480 million French protein snack market [1]. 

Proteo Crisps—oven-baked, legume-based, high-protein crisps featuring short ingredient lists, sustainable compostable packaging, and a strong Nutri-Score A/B rating—will initially target the **Health-conscious office workers** and **Weekly sport practitioners** segments [1]. By bypassing a strictly niche vegan positioning and instead leaning into mainstream everyday snacking, Verdo Foods will position Proteo Crisps as the smart, delicious bridge between indulgence and nutrition ("Le plaisir sain") [1]. 

The launch will focus initially on the Paris, Lyon, and Lille regions, targeting 1,500 points of sale in year one and scaling to a 2% value share of the French protein snack category within 24 months [1].

## 3. Market and competitors

### Market overview
France is the second largest savoury snack market in Europe (€2.9 billion in 2024) and the fastest-growing market for protein snacks in Southern Europe [1]. The protein snack category stands at €480 million, growing at an impressive 11% per year [1]. Plant-based products represent 18% of this category [1]. Furthermore, 27% of French adults bought a protein snack in the last three months [1]. Supermarkets and hypermarkets dominate distribution (68%), followed by specialized sport and organic shops (17%), online channels (9%), and gyms/vending (6%) [1]. French consumers are highly responsive to the Nutri-Score system, which was created in France [1].

### Customer needs
French consumers seeking protein snacks demand products that do not sacrifice taste for nutrition. They look for clean labels (no palm oil, short ingredient lists), transparent nutritional ratings (Nutri-Score A or B), sustainable packaging, and everyday versatility rather than overly clinical, bodybuilder-centric formulations [1].

## 5. Strategy

* **Target Segment:** Primary focus on **Health-conscious office workers** and **Weekly sport practitioners**, while capturing organic demand from **Vegans**. This avoids a restrictive niche and aligns with Verdo Foods' mission to make plant-based protein an everyday snack [1].
* **Positioning:** The guilt-free, delicious, everyday crispy snack that marries superior plant-based nutrition with authentic culinary taste. 
* **Value Proposition:** "15g of plant-based protein, 6 clean ingredients, compostable packaging, and a Nutri-Score A/B—without compromising on the crispy crunch of traditional chips."
* **Pricing Direction:** Positioned slightly above supermarket private labels and parallel to mainstream health brands like Gerblé, justifying a premium through superior nutritional density (15g protein) and clean ingredients.
* **Channels:** 
  * *Mass Retail:* Via a national food distributor targeting 1,500 points of sale in supermarkets and hypermarkets in the Paris, Lyon, and Lille regions [1].
  * *Specialized:* Direct-to-store supply for organic shops and select urban gyms [1].
  * *Digital:* Localized French e-commerce shop [1].
* **Rationale:** Supermarkets control 68% of the French protein snack market [1]. Launching through major retail partners in key urban hubs (Paris, Lyon, Lille) maximizes visibility among busy office workers and active consumers, while digital and gym channels build early brand credibility.

## 6. Campaign

* **Concept:** *“Le croustillant sans compromis”* (The crunch without compromise). The campaign challenges the preconception that healthy snacks taste like cardboard or are meant exclusively for hardcore athletes.
* **Tagline (French):** *"Proteo Crisps : Le plaisir sain qui a du punch."*
* **Key Messages per Channel:**
  * *Supermarkets / Point of Sale (POS):* Highlight the prominent **Nutri-Score A** badge, "15g de protéines," and the France-exclusive *Herbes de Provence* flavour.
  * *Digital / Social Media (Instagram, TikTok):* Focus on office "4 PM cravings" and post-workout recovery, showing real consumers enjoying the snack during daily routines.
  * *Gyms & Specialized Shops:* Emphasize the clean plant-based protein profile (pea protein and chickpea flour) with zero artificial additives [1].
* **Content Ideas:**
  * "Blind Taste Test" videos comparing Proteo Crisps against traditional potato chips on French streets.
  * Quick-format recipe ideas and snack pairings for midday office breaks.
  * Behind-the-scenes content highlighting Bologna food-tech craftsmanship and sustainable, compostable packaging [1].
* **Visual Direction:** Bright, vibrant, and appetizing color palettes matching the flavours (e.g., herb green for Rosemary/Herbes de Provence, warm paprika red). Clean, modern typography emphasizing transparency, sustainability, and Italian culinary heritage paired with the official French Nutri-Score emblem.
