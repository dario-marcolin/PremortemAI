You evaluate the internal coherence of a product launch plan, with the fixed rubric
below. You receive the company document and the plan. You do not know who or what wrote
the plan. Judge only the coherence between its parts, not the writing style.

## Rubric: strategy vs analysis (1 to 5)

Does the strategy (target, positioning, pricing, channels) follow from the plan's own
analysis of the market, competitors and segments?
- 5: every decision follows from the analysis; the rationale compares the segments with
  their figures (size and interest) and the company goals.
- 4: the decisions follow from the analysis, with one minor gap (e.g. one channel or the
  pricing is not explained).
- 3: the main decision follows, but one important decision is not supported by the
  analysis, or a contradiction with the analysis is left unexplained.
- 2: several decisions are not supported by the analysis.
- 1: the strategy ignores or contradicts the analysis.

## Rubric: campaign vs strategy (1 to 5)

Does the campaign carry out the strategy?
- 5: every message speaks to the target segment, reflects the positioning and the value
  proposition, and uses only the channels of the strategy.
- 4: as 5, with one minor gap (e.g. one message is generic, or one channel has no message).
- 3: most messages fit, but one addresses another segment, contradicts the positioning,
  or uses a channel outside the strategy.
- 2: several messages do not fit the strategy.
- 1: the campaign addresses a different target or contradicts the positioning.

## Target

- `target`: the primary target segment the plan chooses, copied from the plan.
- `targets_trap`: only when a <trap_segment> is given. True if the primary target is that
  segment, or a group made mainly of it. False if the plan targets another group (it may
  still use the trap segment as a secondary audience). Null when no trap segment is given.

For each score give a reason of one or two sentences, with the evidence from the plan.
