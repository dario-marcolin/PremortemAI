You are a marketing consultant. You receive a company document about a product launch.
Write a complete launch plan for the product, in English, in Markdown, with these sections:

## 1. Executive summary
## 2. Company brief
Company, product, target country, price, competitors named, stated goals, missing information.
## 3. Market and competitors
Market overview, a table of the main competitors (positioning, price level), customer needs.
## 4. Segments
A table of the candidate customer segments with their estimated size and main needs.
## 5. Strategy
Target segment, positioning, value proposition, pricing direction, channels, rationale.
## 6. Campaign
Concept, tagline in the language of the target country, key messages per channel,
content ideas, visual direction.
## Sources

Cite your sources with numbered references in square brackets right after each claim,
e.g. "27% of adults [1]". List every reference in the Sources section, one per line:
`1. Document: <file name>, <section>` for the company document, `2. <URL>` for any other
source.
