# Launch plan: nordbrew_de

**Kaffeekontor Nord ApS** · launch in Germany · generated on 2026-11-11

## 1. Executive summary


Kaffeekontor Nord should launch Nordbrew Oat Cold Brew in Germany for commuters, adults who buy coffee on the go at least weekly. They are the largest group, with 11.4M adults (22% of adults 18-64) and 34% purchase intent. Specialty coffee enthusiasts show 76% intent but number 0.5M, students show 41% at 2.6M, and office workers 27% at 8.9M. Nordbrew is positioned as the chilled, organic, plant-based cold brew with no added sugar, unlike sweetened milk-based Emmi and Starbucks. Channels are petrol stations, convenience stores, train-station kiosks and supermarkets, with transit out-of-home ads and short-video social media. The campaign "Der Weg ist dein Moment" turns the commute into a short pause. Tagline: "Cold Brew für unterwegs. Ohne Zuckerzusatz."

No blocking issue is open. Most competitor prices are unknown, so 2.49 euros stays provisional pending a shelf-price check. The wholesaler deal and kiosk pilot are unconfirmed. Market and segment figures come from one unverified company document.

## 3. Market and competitors

By channel, supermarkets and discounters account for 54% of sales, petrol stations and convenience stores 24%, train-station and airport kiosks 13%, and online and other 9% [1]. The document calls Germany Europe's largest RTD coffee market and oat drinks its fastest-growing plant-based drink category [2]. The 3% value share goal equals about 18.3 million euros, derived from the 2024 category size [2, 1]. Web research found few German prices. The only German chilled RTD price point is Emmi Caffè Latte Macchiato at 2.99 euros per 230 ml cup [3]. Nordbrew's planned 2.49 euros per 250 ml can (deposit excluded) is below that. ## 5. Strategy


**Target:** SEG2 Commuters. Commuters in Germany: adults who buy coffee on the go at least weekly. They mostly buy at petrol stations, kiosks and supermarkets near transport hubs.

**Positioning.** For German commuters who want a cold coffee on the go without the sugar, Nordbrew Oat Cold Brew is the chilled, organic, plant-based cold brew in a can. It is cold brewed for 18 hours from single-origin Arabica, with no added sugar (2.1 g per 100 ml). Unlike the sweetened milk-based leaders Emmi and Starbucks, it offers a lighter, lower-sugar and vegan alternative. Unlike niche oat players such as Coffrigo, it aims to be on the shelf where commuters already shop.

**Value proposition.** A genuine cold brew with oat drink that is easy to pick up on the way: no added sugar, vegan and organic, in a 250 ml can at a price near the 2.50 euro level commuters were tested on.

**Pricing direction.** Keep 2.49 euros (deposit excluded; 2.74 euros with the 0.25 euro Pfand, my arithmetic) as a provisional price, and do not go above it. The only German chilled price point is Emmi Caffè Latte Macchiato at 2.99 euros per 230 ml at one retailer, with multi-buy offers down to 2.70 euros [3]. Nordbrew sits slightly below it per can. Prices for Starbucks, Oatly, Coffrigo and Mövenpick are unknown, and private labels are described as lower-priced [4]. Do not try to match the discounters. Before listing negotiations, run the planned shelf-price check across German retailers and kiosks and confirm 2.49 euros. Hold it if comparable cold brews sell at similar levels. Lower it only if the check shows that commuters' reference prices are clearly lower.

**Channels:** Petrol stations and convenience stores, Train-station kiosks, Supermarkets, Transit out-of-home ads, Short-video social media

**Rationale.** Commuters are the best fit once size, interest and accessibility are read together. Size: SEG2 has 11.4M adults (22% of adults 18-64), against 0.5M enthusiasts (1%), 2.6M students (5%) and 8.9M office workers (17%) [13]. Interest: the enthusiasts have the highest purchase intent (76%), but at 0.5M they are too small for the goals. The 3% value share is about 18.3M euros [2, 1], and 3,500 points of sale need mass retail. Commuter intent is 34%, ahead of office workers (27%) and below students (41%) [13]. As an illustration only (my arithmetic, not source figures, and intent definitions may differ between groups), intent times size gives roughly 0.4M for enthusiasts, 3.9M for commuters, 1.1M for students and 2.4M for office workers. Commuters therefore have the largest pool of likely buyers. Accessibility: the enthusiasts buy mainly in specialty cafés and online [13]. Online and other channels are only 9% of category sales [1], and the company's online shop is only 12% of its sales. Commuters buy where the category sells: petrol stations and convenience stores 24%, train-station and airport kiosks 13%, supermarkets and discounters 54% [1]. This matches the company's ongoing wholesaler talks and the possible kiosk pilot of about 400 kiosks. Competition: Emmi and Starbucks are widely available but sweetened, milk-based [4]. A low-sugar, plant-based can is a clear difference, and plant-based products already hold 14% of a category worth 610M euros that grew about 7% a year [1]. Coffrigo is a direct concept match (oat, cold brew, no added sugar), but its retail reach is unknown [9]. Claims used: no added sugar, vegan, organic, 18-hour single-origin cold brew, all from the product description. I deliberately avoid the "52% preferred Nordbrew" taste-test claim because it is Danish, company-run and against one unnamed competitor [12]. I also avoid a "less bitter" claim, since 22% of testers found the Original too bitter [12]. Assumptions and risks: all market and segment figures come from one company document that the analysis notes as unverified by independent sources. The kiosk pilot and the wholesaler deal are not confirmed, so the supermarket listings carry the 3,500-point goal. Discounters, which are part of the 54% supermarket and discounter channel, are not targeted because private labels compete on price there. The Vanilla variant could suit commuters who find Original bitter, but that is my assumption and needs testing in Germany. Specialty enthusiasts can be a secondary audience, through social media, to build credibility, but not the main target. Oatly's German availability is unconfirmed, so I do not rely on it as a competitor in the positioning.

Supporting sources: [12, 2, 1, 13, 4, 3, 9]


## 6. Campaign


**Concept.** "Der Weg ist dein Moment": the daily commute becomes a small pause with a can of cold brew that has nothing to hide. Every execution shows the journey in short, real moments (platform, petrol station, tram) and ends on the can and its simple facts: 18 hours cold brewed, no added sugar, vegan, organic. The idea expresses the positioning by making Nordbrew the chilled, plant-based cold coffee that is simply there on the way, where commuters already shop.

**Tagline:** *Cold Brew für unterwegs. Ohne Zuckerzusatz.*

| Petrol stations and convenience stores | Kalt gestellt. Schnell gegriffen. | Nordbrew Oat Cold Brew: 18 Stunden kalt gebrüht, mit Hafer-Drink, ohne Zuckerzusatz (2,1 g pro 100 ml). Vegan, bio, in der handlichen 250-ml-Dose. Jetzt im Kühlregal an der Kasse. |

| Supermarkets | Cold Brew aus Aarhus. Jetzt im Kühlregal. | Sorte: Single-Origin-Arabica aus Kolumbien und Äthiopien, 18 Stunden kalt gebrüht und mit Hafer-Drink abgerundet. Ohne Zuckerzusatz, vegan, bio-zertifiziert. Die 250-ml-Dose findest du im Kühlregal. |
| Transit out-of-home ads | 18 Stunden Cold Brew. 0 g Zuckerzusatz. | Nordbrew Oat Cold Brew. Vegan, bio, ohne Zuckerzusatz. Jetzt am Bahnhof und an der Tankstelle. |
| Short-video social media | Dein Weg zur Arbeit hat ein Upgrade verdient. | Bahnsteig, Dose auf, ein kalter Schluck. Nordbrew Oat Cold Brew: 18 Stunden kalt gebrüht, ohne Zuckerzusatz, vegan und bio. Zeig uns deinen Pendelmoment mit #MeinNordbrewMoment. |

**Content ideas**

- Cold-storage cooler branding and shelf-strip wobblers for petrol stations and convenience stores, with the line 'Kalt gestellt. Schnell gegriffen.' and a simple fact panel (18 h cold brew, no added sugar, vegan, organic).
- Branded chilled display units and counter stickers in train-station kiosks, designed for a 5-second grab-and-go purchase, if the kiosk pilot is confirmed.
- Transit out-of-home campaign in station posters, platform screens and tram/S-Bahn interiors in major German commuter cities, with large can visual and the facts '18 h cold brewed, 0 g added sugar'.
- Series of 15-second vertical videos showing commute moments (platform, bike, tram) ending with the can opening sound and the tagline.
- User-generated video challenge #MeinNordbrewMoment inviting commuters to share their daily commute pause, with reposts on the brand account.
- Short 'How it's made' video clips: 18-hour cold brew, Colombian and Ethiopian single-origin Arabica, from Aarhus roastery, told in 20 seconds.
- Supermarket chilled-shelf navigation: shelf-edge strips and secondary placement near transport-hub stores, with clear 'no added sugar, vegan, organic' call-outs.
- Launch sampling of chilled cans at selected busy station entrances during morning rush hour in launch month.

**Visual direction.** Clean Nordic style: lots of white space, cool and calm. Palette of deep cold-brew brown and off-white, with a fresh glacier-blue accent for the 'cold' cue and a small organic-green touch for the EU organic label. Imagery: real commute scenes in soft morning light (platforms, tram windows, petrol station coolers), condensation on a chilled aluminium can held in one hand, close-ups of the can opening. Typography: bold, simple sans-serif with short headlines legible in 2 seconds from a moving train. Packaging cues: matte aluminium can with a clear large 'Oat Cold Brew' name, icon row for 'no added sugar', vegan and organic, and the EU organic logo; the key facts always readable on the front. Photography and video should be natural and unhurried, with no gym or performance imagery.
