# Launch plan: fakturo_it

## 1. Executive summary


Kasa Software, a Polish company with 38,000 paying customers in Poland [1], will launch **Fakturo** in Italy in January 2027, at the start of the tax year [3]. Fakturo creates FatturaPA e-invoices, sends them to the SdI, and estimates taxes under the flat-rate regime (*regime forfettario*) after every invoice [2]. The board's goals are 12,000 paying customers in 24 months, monthly cancellations below 3%, and at least 3 professional-association partnerships in year 1 [3]. The year-1 marketing budget is 600,000 euros [7].

**Recommendation in brief:**
- **Target:** flat-rate freelancers who do not yet use an invoicing app (1.06 million people, 23% purchase intent, the highest of any group) [5]. 58% of them say they "do not understand how much tax they will owe" [5].
- **Positioning:** Fakturo is the invoicing app that shows what you owe in tax, not just an invoicing tool. It is simpler and more focused than the broad market leader Fatture in Cloud, and it does more than the free tax-agency tool, which has no tax estimate [6].
- **Price:** keep 9 euros per month and push the 90-euro annual plan (7.50 euros per month), which sits close to the 7.20-euro average price paid today [2][4]. Do not launch at a lower list price. Rely on the 30-day trial and association discounts instead.
- **Channels:** search and app-store acquisition, educational content, and partnerships with associations and accountants, all within the online-only sales model [7].
- **Campaign:** "Ogni fattura, le tue tasse chiare." The tax estimate is the hero feature.
- **Risks to manage:**
  - The F24 payment feature, requested by 41% of beta users, is not available yet [2].
  - Monthly churn in Poland is 4%, above the Italian target of under 3% [1][3].
  - Beta retention was 68% after 3 months [2].

The 12,000-customer goal looks reachable. It equals about 1.1% of the primary segment and about 4.9% of its intent-weighted pool (own calculation from [5]). The main uncertainties are acquisition cost and retention, and the document gives no data on either for Italy.

## 3. Market and competitors


### Market overview

- Italy has **3.4 million** freelancers with a VAT number. **1.9 million** of them are in the flat-rate regime [4].
- **44%** of flat-rate freelancers already use an invoicing app, so about 56% (1.06 million) do not [4][5].
- The average monthly price paid for an invoicing app is **7.20 euros**. Fakturo's 9 euros is 25% above that (own calculation from [2][4]).
- Mandatory e-invoicing for flat-rate freelancers since 2024 created a group of people who now need a tool they never used before [3].
- Non-users mostly rely on free tax-agency tools or on their accountant [5].

### Competitors

| Competitor | Positioning | Price level |
|---|---|---|
| **Fatture in Cloud** | Market leader with broad features for small businesses [6] | 4 to 30 euros per month [6] |
| **Agenzia delle Entrate free tool** | Basic invoicing, no tax estimate [6] | Free [6] |
| **Accountant-provided software** | Often bundled in the accountant's fee [6] | Often "free" to the user, included in the fee [6] |
| **Other freelance apps** | Smaller apps focused on the flat-rate regime [6] | 5 to 12 euros per month [6] |
| **Fakturo (for comparison)** | Invoicing plus a live tax estimate for the flat-rate regime [2] | 9 euros per month or 90 per year [2] |

Fakturo is priced mid-range against the focused apps (5 to 12 euros) and above the 7.20-euro market average. Its distinctive point against the free tool and the leader is the tax estimate, which the free tool lacks [6]. The document does not say whether competitors offer one.

### Customer needs

- **Clarity on taxes.** 58% of non-app users do not understand how much tax they will owe [5].
- **Compliance without effort.** They must issue FatturaPA invoices and send them to the SdI [2][3].
- **Simplicity.** These are new users who today rely on free tools or their accountant [5].
- **Expense tracking and accountant handover.** Fakturo covers these with receipt capture and the accountant export [2].
- **Paying tax instalments in the same app.** 41% of beta users requested F24 [2].

## 4. Segments


Purchase intent was measured for "an app that creates e-invoices and estimates your taxes, at 9 euros per month" [5]. The last column is own calculation (size × intent). It is indicative only, because stated intent usually overstates real purchases.

| Segment | Size | Purchase intent | Intent-weighted pool | Main needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Flat-rate freelancers, no app yet** | 1.06 million [5] | 23% [5] | about 244,000 | Understand taxes (58%), compliance, simplicity, replacing free tools or accountant dependence [5] |
| **B. Flat-rate freelancers with an app** | 0.84 million [5] | 9% [5] | about 76,000 | Switching reason needed, such as a better tax estimate. They already pay about 7.20 euros on average [4] |
| **C. Ordinary-regime freelancers (with an accountant)** | 1.5 million [5] | 6% [5] | 90,000 | Accountant-led workflow. Fakturo's tax estimate targets the flat-rate regime, so the fit is weak [2] |
| **D. Craft businesses with one owner (*artigiani*)** | 0.9 million [5] | 11% [5] | about 99,000 | Not described in the document. Regime and needs to be researched |

**Reading the table:**
- Segment A has by far the highest intent and the largest pool, and its need matches Fakturo's core feature.
- Segment D has the second-highest intent but is a poorly understood group. Test it with a small budget.
- Segment C is the weakest: low intent, and the tax estimate is built for the flat-rate regime [2][5].
- Segment B is a conversion fight against incumbents, with the lowest cost of explanation but a high cost of switching.

## 5. Strategy


### Target segment

- **Primary:** Segment A, flat-rate freelancers who do not yet use an invoicing app (1.06 million, 23% intent) [5].
- **Entry beachheads inside A:** designers and IT consultants, the two groups already in association talks [7].
- **Secondary, test and learn:** Segment D (*artigiani*) with a limited budget, once its regime and needs are clarified [5].
- **Opportunistic:** Segment B, only through comparison and switching content.
- **Deprioritised:** Segment C [5].

### Positioning

*For flat-rate freelancers who find taxes confusing, Fakturo is the invoicing app that tells you after every invoice how much tax you will owe.*

- Against **Fatture in Cloud**: Fakturo is not the broadest tool but the clearest. It is built for the flat-rate freelancer, not for every small business [6].
- Against the **free tax-agency tool**: it also does the one thing the free tool does not, the tax estimate [6].
- Against **accountant software**: Fakturo gives the freelancer daily control and an export that makes the accountant's work easier, so it complements the accountant instead of replacing them [2][6].

### Value proposition

"Invoice in minutes, send it to the SdI automatically, and always know how much to set aside for taxes." Supporting proof points are the 4.4/5 beta rating and 68% three-month activity [2], to be used carefully as a beta sample of 250 users.

### Pricing direction

- **Keep 9 euros per month** as the list price [2]. No evidence in the document supports a lower price: intent (23% in segment A) was measured at 9 euros [5].
- **Lead with the annual plan** at 90 euros (7.50 euros per month, about 17% below the monthly price). It is close to the 7.20-euro market average [4], and it supports the churn goal by reducing monthly cancellation decisions.
- **Use the 30-day trial** as the main price-risk reducer [2].
- **Association discounts** for members, as already discussed with two associations [7]. Keep them limited and tied to the partnership agreements.
- **Test, don't guess.** In the first 3 months, run a controlled test of a first-year introductory offer on part of the traffic. Compare conversion and 3-month retention before changing the list price.
- **Economics to check** (own illustration, assuming a 9-euro monthly plan and no margin data):
  - At 3% monthly churn the average customer lifetime is about 33 months, or about 300 euros of revenue.
  - At 4% churn it is about 25 months, or about 225 euros.
  - If 600,000 euros brought in half of the 12,000 target (6,000 customers), CAC would be about 100 euros, or roughly 11 months of revenue before costs [3][7].

  Acquisition targets must be set with Kasa's margin data, which the document lacks.

### Channels

Sales are online only (website, App Store, Google Play) [7]. A proposed split of the 600,000-euro year-1 budget:

| Channel | Share | Euros | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search ads and app-store optimisation and ads | 35% | 210,000 | Capture intent: "fattura elettronica forfettario", "quante tasse pago forfettario" |
| Content, SEO and video | 20% | 120,000 | Educate on taxes and e-invoicing, and build free tools such as a tax calculator |
| Partnerships and community (associations, accountants, creators) | 15% | 90,000 | Trust and credibility. At least 3 association deals [3] |
| Paid social (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube) | 15% | 90,000 | Reach new freelancers, with retargeting of site visitors |
| Referral programme and CRM or email onboarding | 5% | 30,000 | Retention and word of mouth |
| PR and launch moment | 5% | 30,000 | Awareness around the January start of the tax year |
| Measurement and contingency | 5% | 30,000 | Tests and reallocation |

### Rationale

- Segment A has the highest intent (23%) and the clearest unmet need (58% do not understand their taxes) [5].
- Fakturo's distinctive feature, the live tax estimate, speaks directly to that need, and the free tool lacks it [2][6].
- The tax-year start in January 2027 is a natural moment of attention, when freelancers set up their year [3].
- Associations give credibility to a foreign brand with no Italian awareness, and they serve the partnership goal [3][7].
- The accountant is influential for non-app users [5]. The accountant export lets Fakturo be positioned as a helper, not a threat [2].

### Risks and mitigations

- **F24 gap.** 41% of beta users want it [2]. Put it on the roadmap, communicate it only when a date is firm, and do not advertise it before it ships.
- **Retention.** 68% active after 3 months in the beta equates to roughly 12% monthly drop-off if constant (own calculation from [2]). This is far worse than the 3% churn target [3], although "inactive" is not the same as "cancelled". Measure real paid churn in the first cohorts, and invest in onboarding (first invoice within 24 hours) and the annual plan.
- **Tax-estimate accuracy.** Validate with Kasa's tax experts and include a clear "estimate, not tax advice" notice.
- **Local trust.** Use Italian-language support, local testimonials and association endorsements.

### KPIs

- 12,000 paying customers at month 24 [3].
- Monthly churn below 3% [3].
- At least 3 association partnerships in year 1 [3].
- Trial-to-paid conversion, share on the annual plan, CAC by channel, and first-invoice activation within 7 days (internal targets to be set).

## 6. Campaign


### Concept

**"Le tue tasse, chiare ad ogni fattura."** The campaign turns an anxious topic (58% do not know what tax they owe [5]) into a moment of relief. Every time the freelancer issues an invoice, Fakturo shows the tax estimate update. The core idea is the *"numero tranquillo"*: a clear number that replaces uncertainty. Tone: friendly, plain-spoken, a little humorous, never bureaucratic. The brand story can draw on the founders' background as former tax consultants [1].

### Tagline (Italian)

**"Fakturo. Ogni fattura, le tue tasse chiare."**
Alternative for testing: "Fattura e sai subito quanto devi."

### Key messages per channel

| Channel | Key message (Italian example, with English meaning) |
|---|---|
| **Search ads** | "Fattura elettronica forfettario: scopri subito quante tasse paghi. Prova 30 giorni gratis." (E-invoicing for flat-rate: see your taxes right away. 30 days free.) |
| **App stores** | "Fatture elettroniche e stima delle tasse in un'unica app." (E-invoices and tax estimate in one app.) Use the beta rating only once real store ratings exist. |
| **Website and landing pages** | "Emetti la fattura, invia allo SdI, sai quanto mettere da parte." (Issue the invoice, send to the SdI, know how much to set aside.) Interactive demo with a live tax estimate. |
| **Social (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube)** | "Hai fatturato 3.000 €. Quanto è tuo davvero?" (You invoiced 3,000 euros. How much is really yours?) Short videos that answer it. |
| **LinkedIn and association channels** | "Pensato per i forfettari: meno burocrazia, più lavoro." (Built for flat-rate freelancers: less paperwork, more work.) Member offers for designers and IT consultants [7]. |
| **Accountants and partners** | "Il tuo cliente arriva con i dati in ordine: esportazione pronta per il commercialista." (Your client arrives with organised data: export ready for the accountant.) [2] |
| **Email and in-app onboarding** | "Emetti la tua prima fattura in 5 minuti." (Issue your first invoice in 5 minutes.) Nudge toward the annual plan before the trial ends. |

### Content ideas

- **Free tax calculator** for flat-rate freelancers on the website, as a lead magnet leading to the trial.
- **Explainer series** "Forfettario in 60 secondi": e-invoicing, SdI, and how to read the tax estimate.
- **Case stories** of beta users (designers, IT consultants), with their consent [2][7].
- **Checklist and webinar** for January: "Parti bene con l'anno fiscale" (start the tax year well), with association partners [3][7].
- **Comparison pages**: Fakturo vs free tool vs accountant software, written honestly [6].
- **Receipt-photo challenge** on social: how fast can you log an expense [2].
- **Referral campaign** "Porta un collega": a free month for both sides.
- **Accountant guide**: how the export makes bookkeeping easier [2].

### Visual direction

- Clean, bright and reassuring, with one signature colour (for example a warm green or teal for "tranquillity") against white.
- The **tax-estimate number** is the hero visual: large, clear numerals on a phone screen, updating as an invoice is created.
- Real freelancers in their workspaces (designers, developers, craftspeople), not stock offices.
- Simple illustrations and friendly icons that turn tax jargon into everyday images.
- Consistent Italian-language UI screenshots, with FatturaPA and SdI shown plainly, never hidden.

### Phasing

1. **Oct–Dec 2026, pre-launch:** waitlist, tax-calculator content, signing association partnerships, beta testimonials.
2. **January 2027, launch:** PR moment, search and social burst, association webinars, 30-day trial.
3. **Feb–Jun 2027:** optimisation by channel, annual-plan push, onboarding improvements, price-offer test results.
4. **H2 2027 onwards:** referral growth, expansion to *artigiani* if the test succeeds, and F24 communication once it ships.
