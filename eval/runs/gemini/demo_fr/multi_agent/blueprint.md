# Launch Blueprint: Proteo Crisps

**Verdo Foods S.r.l.** · launch in France · generated on 2026-11-11

## 1. Executive summary

Verdo Foods S.r.l. is launching Proteo Crisps in France, targeting weekly sport practitioners. This segment was selected because it represents 17.5 million adults (34% of adults) with a 41% purchase intent, providing the necessary reach compared to vegans and strict vegetarians, who showed a 72% purchase intent but represent only 1.3 million adults (2.5% of adults). Proteo Crisps are positioned as a plant-based, oven-baked chickpea and pea protein snack delivering 15 g of protein with clean ingredients. Distribution and marketing focus on supermarkets and hypermarkets, specialised sport and organic shops, online, gyms and vending, and Instagram. The campaign bridges active lifestyle needs and clean nutrition using the tagline "Le croustillant végétal riche en protéines pour vos pauses sportives." No unresolved blocking issues remain. Key open gaps affecting launch execution are establishing product pricing, selecting the national distributor, and finalizing the marketing budget.

## 5. Strategy

**Target:** SEG2 Weekly sport practitioners. Weekly sport practitioners, representing adults engaging in weekly physical activity.

**Positioning.** For weekly sport practitioners seeking convenient high-protein snacking, Proteo Crisps offer a plant-based, oven-baked chickpea and pea protein crisp with 15 g of protein and clean ingredients, standing apart from traditional wellness brands and lower-price private labels.

**Value proposition.** Convenient high-protein snacking and muscle recovery support with clean ingredients and no additives.

**Pricing direction.** Pricing direction is currently unknown because competitor prices are not detailed in the analysis [data_quality_notes]; pricing must be decided after benchmarking against competitors like Barebells and supermarket private labels.

**Channels:** Supermarkets and hypermarkets, Specialised sport and organic shops, Online, Gyms and vending, Instagram

**Rationale.** SEG2 is chosen as the primary target because it is the largest consumer group with 17.5 million adults (34% of adults) and a strong 41% purchase intent [4, 5]. Although SEG1 has a higher purchase intent at 72%, it represents only 1.3 million adults (2.5% of adults) [4, 5], which is insufficient on its own to achieve the ambitious company goal of listing in at least 1,500 points of sale and reaching a 2% value share of the 480 million euro protein snack category [1]. Channels are selected to match the market distribution where supermarkets and hypermarkets drive 68% of sales, specialised sport and organic shops 17%, online 9%, and gyms and vending 6% [1].

## 6. Campaign

**Concept.** Proteo Crisps bridge the gap between active lifestyle needs and clean nutrition for weekly sports practitioners. By highlighting 15 g of protein and an oven-baked chickpea recipe with no additives, the campaign positions Proteo Crisps as the ideal plant-based snack for convenient high-protein snacking.

**Tagline:** *Le croustillant végétal riche en protéines pour vos pauses sportives.*

| Channel | Headline | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Supermarkets and hypermarkets | Vos encas protéinés au rayon chips | Découvrez Proteo Crisps : 15 g de protéines par sachet, sans huile de palme et sans additifs pour allier gourmandise et nutrition. |
| Specialised sport and organic shops | L'alternative végétale pour vos pauses | Des chips de pois chiches et protéines de pois cuites au four, certifiées vegan et sans gluten, avec seulement six ingrédients. |
| Online | Commandez vos encas protéinés en ligne | Profitez de 15 g de protéines par sachet avec Proteo Crisps, le snack savoureux et clean livré directement chez vous. |
| Gyms and vending | Le snack idéal après l'entraînement | 15 g de protéines par sachet avec Proteo Crisps, disponibles dès maintenant en distributeur. |
| Instagram | Du sport, du goût, et 15 g de protéines | Intégrez Proteo Crisps à votre routine sportive. Des ingrédients simples, sans additifs, pour votre pause. |

**Content ideas**

- In-store tasting stands in major French supermarkets during launch month highlighting the 15 g of protein and clean ingredients
- Partner displays in specialised sport and organic shops featuring athlete testimonies focused on convenient snacking
- Targeted Instagram video ads demonstrating the 6 simple ingredients and the oven-baked chickpea recipe for active adults
- Sponsored vending machine wraps in partner gyms showcasing quick and easy high-protein snacking between training sessions
- Unboxing and tasting video collaborations with French fitness and lifestyle creators on Instagram
- Digital banner ads on e-commerce grocery channels emphasizing the certified vegan and gluten-free attributes

**Visual direction.** Clean, energetic and natural aesthetic featuring earthy green and warm terracotta tones to reflect the chickpea and pea protein ingredients. Photography highlights dynamic action shots of weekly sports practitioners combined with crisp, appetizing product packaging showing the 15 g of protein callout clearly.
