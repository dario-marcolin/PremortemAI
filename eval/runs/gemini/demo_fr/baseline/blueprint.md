## 1. Executive summary

This document outlines the strategic launch plan for Verdo Foods’ flagship product, **Proteo Crisps**, into the French market in March 2026. Building on its profitable growth in Italy, Verdo Foods aims to capture a 2% value share of the French protein snack category within 24 months, securing listings in at least 1,500 points of sale in year one. 

By positioning Proteo Crisps as an everyday, great-tasting, clean-label savoury snack rather than a niche athletic or vegan product, the launch targets health-conscious office workers and weekly sport practitioners. Leveraging a strong Nutri-Score A profile, sustainable compostable packaging, and a dedicated new flavour (*Herbes de Provence*), the campaign will combine targeted digital marketing, influencer partnerships, and impactful in-store merchandising across the Paris, Lyon, and Lille regions.

## 3. Market and competitors

### Market overview
The French savoury snack market reached €2.9 billion in 2024 [1]. Within this, the protein snack category stands at €480 million, demonstrating a robust annual growth rate of 11% between 2021 and 2024 [1]. Plant-based options account for 18% of the protein snack category, and 27% of French adults purchased a protein snack in the three months prior to the 2024 survey [1]. Supermarkets and hypermarkets dominate distribution channels at 68%, followed by specialised sport and organic shops (17%), online (9%), and gyms/vending (6%) [1].

### Customer needs
French consumers seek snacks that combine functional health benefits (such as high protein and clean ingredient lists) with uncompromised indulgence and taste. Familiarity with the French-originating Nutri-Score makes front-of-pack nutritional transparency a major driver of consumer trust and purchase decisions. Furthermore, ecological consciousness demands sustainable, compostable packaging without sacrificing product shelf-life.

## 5. Strategy

*   **Target Segment:** Primary focus on **Health-conscious office workers** and **Weekly sport practitioners**. While vegans display the highest purchase intent (72%), their market size is small (1.3 million) [1]. The company's mission is to make plant-based protein an *everyday* snack for the mass market [1]. Targeting office workers and sports enthusiasts captures a massive combined audience (26.5 million adults) with high absolute purchase volume potential.
*   **Positioning:** Position Proteo Crisps as the modern, delicious, everyday crunchy snack that bridges the gap between junk food indulgence and functional sports nutrition. 
*   **Value Proposition:** "The guilt-free crunch." 15g of natural plant-based protein per bag, Nutri-Score A (for Sea Salt, Rosemary, and Herbes de Provence), only six clean ingredients, zero palm oil, and 100% compostable packaging [1].
*   **Pricing Direction:** Position competitively just above supermarket private labels and standard potato crisps, but slightly below premium imported specialty protein bars (like Barebells), reflecting an accessible mass-market healthy snack tier.
*   **Channels:** 
    *   Mass-market retail via a national food distributor targeting 1,500+ supermarkets in the Paris, Lyon, and Lille regions [1].
    *   Direct-to-consumer delivery for organic shops, selected gyms, and the localized e-commerce website [1].
*   **Rationale:** Utilizing standard supermarket logistics (supported by a 9-month shelf life) [1] ensures broad visibility where 68% of protein snack sales occur [1]. Focusing initial geographic rollouts on Paris, Lyon, and Lille maximizes logistical efficiency and population density capture.

## 6. Campaign

*   **Concept:** *"Le Crunch Malin"* (The Smart Crunch) — contrasting the mindless snacking of traditional chips with a smart, flavorful, protein-packed choice that fits seamlessly into a busy lifestyle.
*   **Tagline (French):** *"Le croustillant sain qui a du pep's."* (The healthy, punchy crunch.)
*   **Key Messages per Channel:**
    *   **Supermarkets (POS / Shelf):** Highlight Nutri-Score A, "15g de protéines", and the new *Herbes de Provence* local flavour.
    *   **Digital & Social Media (Instagram / TikTok):** Target office workers and active urbanites with messaging on afternoon energy slumps, clean ingredients (only 6 ingredients), and zero guilt.
    *   **Gyms & Specialized Sports Channels:** Emphasize plant-based muscle recovery and clean protein content (30% more than leading Italian alternatives) [1].
*   **Content Ideas:**
    *   Short-form video content showing office workers swapping greasy potato chips for Proteo Crisps during the 4 PM work slump.
    *   Taste-test comparison reels featuring French influencers comparing Proteo Crisps against traditional potato crisps.
    *   Behind-the-scenes content highlighting Italian culinary heritage (Bologna roots) [1] combined with eco-friendly compostable packaging.
*   **Visual Direction:** Clean, vibrant, and appetizing pastel tones paired with earthy greens to emphasize natural legume ingredients and sustainability. Clear, prominent callouts for the Nutri-Score A badge and the 15g protein metric on the front of the pack [1].
