# Krikit – Company and Product Brief

Launch of Krikit insect-protein dog food in the Netherlands. Internal document for the launch planning team. All companies, people and figures in this document are fictional and used for evaluation only.

## 1. Company overview

Krikit GmbH is an Austrian pet food company founded in 2020 in Graz by Lena Huber, a veterinarian, and Tobias Gruber, a food engineer. It makes dog food with protein from black soldier fly larvae, farmed in Austria on food industry by-products.

In 2024 Krikit had 19 employees and a turnover of 3.4 million euros, 90% in Austria and 10% in Germany. Its products are sold in about 650 pet shops and veterinary clinics and through its own online shop, which accounts for 35% of sales.

## 2. The product: Krikit Adult

Krikit Adult is a complete dry food for adult dogs.

- 30% protein, 60% of it from insect larvae; no chicken, beef or grain.
- Suitable for most dogs with food sensitivities to common proteins (veterinary feedback, no clinical trial).
- Carbon footprint about 60% lower than a beef-based dry food, according to a 2024 life cycle study commissioned by the company.
- Bags of 1.5 kg and 7 kg.
- Price in Austria: 14.90 euros for 1.5 kg, 54.90 euros for 7 kg.

In a feeding test run by the company in Austria in 2024 (120 dogs, 6 weeks), 83% of dogs ate the food readily from the first day, and 76% of owners said they would buy it again.

## 3. Why the Netherlands, and launch goals

The Netherlands has one of the highest shares of dog owners who pay attention to sustainability in Europe, and insect protein is already known to Dutch consumers.

Goals for the Dutch launch, approved by the board in March 2025:

- Reach 1.5 million euros of sales in the Netherlands in the second year.
- Be listed in at least 300 pet shops and veterinary clinics in the first year.
- Reach 4,000 online subscription customers by the end of the second year.

The launch is planned for May 2026.

## 5. Dog owner groups

The Dutch Pet Owner Survey 2025 measured interest in "a complete dry dog food made with insect protein, at a premium price" among dog-owning households.

| Group | Households | Share of dog-owning households | Purchase intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owners of dogs with food sensitivities | 0.29 million | 18% | 39% |
| Sustainability-minded owners already buying premium food | 0.38 million | 24% | 33% |
| Owners buying mainly in supermarkets on price | 0.71 million | 44% | 6% |
| First-time owners (dog for less than 2 years) | 0.22 million | 14% | 21% |

Owners of dogs with food sensitivities mostly buy on the advice of their vet. Sustainability-minded owners buy in pet shop chains and online.

## 6. Competitors

- **Yarrah**: Dutch organic pet food brand, sold in supermarkets and organic shops.
- **Hill's Prescription Diet and Royal Canin Veterinary**: hypoallergenic veterinary foods, sold through vets.
- **Other insect-based brands**: at least two small brands, sold online.
- **Premium brands of pet shop chains**: own brands of the two largest chains.

No detailed price comparison has been carried out.

## 7. Distribution and marketing

Krikit is negotiating with one of the two largest Dutch pet shop chains (about 150 stores) and with a distributor that supplies veterinary clinics. The online shop will ship to the Netherlands from launch. The marketing budget for the first year is 350,000 euros.
