# Kasa Software – Company and Product Brief

Launch of the Fakturo invoicing and tax app in Italy. Internal document for the launch planning team. All companies, people and figures in this document are fictional and used for evaluation only.

## 1. Company overview

Kasa Software sp. z o.o. is a Polish software company founded in 2016 in Kraków by Anna Wiśniewska and Piotr Zając, two former tax consultants. It builds invoicing and bookkeeping apps for freelancers and very small businesses.

In 2024 Kasa had 64 employees, 38,000 paying customers in Poland and a turnover of 7.8 million euros. Customers pay a monthly subscription; 4% of them cancel each month.

## 2. The product: Fakturo

Fakturo is a web and mobile app that creates electronic invoices, sends them to the national e-invoicing system and estimates the taxes the user will owe.

- Electronic invoices in the Italian FatturaPA format, sent to the Sistema di Interscambio (SdI).
- Tax estimate for the flat-rate regime (regime forfettario), updated with every invoice.
- Photo capture of receipts and expense tracking.
- Export for the accountant (commercialista).
- Price: 9 euros per month, or 90 euros per year. 30-day free trial.

The Italian version was tested in 2025 with 250 beta users: 68% were still active after 3 months, and the average rating was 4.4 out of 5. 41% of beta users asked for a feature to pay their tax instalments (F24) from the app, which is not yet available.

## 3. Why Italy, and launch goals

Italy made electronic invoicing mandatory for flat-rate freelancers in 2024, so many of them now need a tool they did not use before.

Goals for the Italian launch, approved by the board in July 2025:

- Reach 12,000 paying customers in Italy within 24 months.
- Keep monthly cancellations below 3%.
- Sign partnerships with at least 3 professional associations in the first year.

The launch is planned for January 2027, at the start of the tax year.

## 5. Customer groups

The Freelance Barometer 2025 measured interest in "an app that creates electronic invoices and estimates your taxes, at 9 euros per month" among freelancers with a VAT number.

| Group | Freelancers | Purchase intent |
|---|---|---|
| Flat-rate freelancers who do not use an invoicing app yet | 1.06 million | 23% |
| Flat-rate freelancers who already use an invoicing app | 0.84 million | 9% |
| Freelancers in the ordinary regime (with an accountant) | 1.5 million | 6% |
| Craft businesses with one owner (artigiani) | 0.9 million | 11% |

Freelancers who do not use an app today mostly use free tools from the tax agency or ask their accountant. 58% of them say they "do not understand how much tax they will owe".

## 6. Competitors

- **Fatture in Cloud**: market leader, broad features for small businesses, plans from 4 to 30 euros per month.
- **Agenzia delle Entrate free tool**: free, basic invoicing, no tax estimate.
- **Accountant-provided software**: often included in the accountant's fee.
- **Other freelance apps**: several smaller apps focused on the flat-rate regime, 5 to 12 euros per month.

## 7. Distribution and marketing

Fakturo will be sold online only: website, App Store and Google Play. Kasa is talking to two professional associations (one for designers, one for IT consultants) about member discounts. The marketing budget for the first year is 600,000 euros.
