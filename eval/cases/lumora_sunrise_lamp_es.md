# Claridade – Company and Product Brief

Launch of the Lumora sunrise alarm lamp in Spain. Internal document for the launch planning team. All companies, people and figures in this document are fictional and used for evaluation only.

## 1. Company overview

Claridade Lda is a Portuguese consumer electronics company founded in 2019 in Porto by Inês Carvalho, an industrial designer, and Rui Antunes, an electronics engineer. It designs smart lighting products for the home and manufactures them through a partner factory in northern Portugal.

In 2024 Claridade had 23 employees and a turnover of 5.1 million euros, 85% in Portugal. Its products are sold in about 420 electronics and home stores in Portugal, on Amazon Portugal and through its own online shop, which accounts for 31% of sales.

## 2. The product: Lumora

Lumora is a bedside lamp that wakes the user with light that grows gradually brighter over 30 minutes, simulating a sunrise, followed by a soft sound.

- Brightness up to 300 lux; colour from deep red to daylight white.
- Sunset mode for falling asleep, 8 natural sounds, FM radio.
- App for iOS and Android; works without the app with physical buttons.
- 2-year warranty, repairable design with replaceable parts.
- Recommended retail price in Portugal: 89 euros.

In a home test run by the company in Portugal in 2024 (180 participants, 4 weeks), 64% said they woke up "more easily" than with their usual alarm, and 71% kept using the lamp after the test. The test had no control group.

Intended price in Spain: 89 to 99 euros.

## 3. Why Spain, and launch goals

Spain is Claridade's closest large market, and the company already ships online orders there. Daylight saving time and late sunrises in western Spain make winter mornings dark until about 8:30.

Goals for the Spanish launch, approved by the board in September 2025:

- Sell 40,000 units in Spain in the first 18 months.
- Be listed in at least 600 stores, including one national electronics chain.
- Reach a 4.3 rating or higher on Amazon Spain within the first year.

The launch is planned for October 2026, before the winter season.

## 5. Consumer groups

The Panel Hogar 2025 measured interest in "a sunrise alarm lamp at about 90 euros" among Spanish adults aged 18 to 64 (about 30 million people).

| Group | Adults | Share of adults 18-64 | Purchase intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adults who have difficulty waking up in winter | 11.4 million | 38% | 24% |
| Shift workers (early or rotating shifts) | 2.1 million | 7% | 29% |
| Parents of children under 10 | 5.3 million | 18% | 17% |
| Students living away from home | 1.2 million | 4% | 12% |

Interest is higher among women (27%) than men (19%). Price is the main reason for not buying: 46% of non-interested adults say 90 euros is too much for an alarm.

## 6. Competitors

- **Philips Wake-up Light**: market leader, sold in all electronics chains, several models from 60 to 200 euros.
- **Lumie**: British brand, premium models, mainly online.
- **Unbranded imports** on online marketplaces, from 25 euros, often with poor reviews.

Claridade has not compared features and prices in detail.

## 7. Distribution and marketing

Claridade sells online in Spain already (about 1,900 units in 2024 without marketing). It is negotiating with one national electronics chain and two department store groups. The marketing budget for the first 18 months is 450,000 euros.
