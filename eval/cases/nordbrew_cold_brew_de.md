# Kaffeekontor Nord – Company and Product Brief

Launch of Nordbrew Oat Cold Brew in Germany. Internal document for the launch planning team. All companies, people and figures in this document are fictional and used for evaluation only.

## 1. Company overview

Kaffeekontor Nord ApS is a Danish coffee roaster founded in 2017 in Aarhus by Signe Holm, a former barista champion, and Jonas Lund, previously a sales manager at a Danish dairy cooperative. The company roasts specialty coffee and sells it to cafés, offices and consumers.

In 2024 the company had 41 employees and a turnover of 9.6 million euros: 70% in Denmark, 30% in Sweden. Its products are sold in about 1,800 points of sale (supermarkets, convenience stores and cafés) and through its own online shop, which accounts for 12% of sales. The company raised 6 million euros in 2025 to launch its ready-to-drink range in Germany.

## 2. The product: Nordbrew Oat Cold Brew

Nordbrew is a cold brew coffee with oat drink, sold chilled in 250 ml cans. It was launched in Denmark in 2023 and now accounts for 38% of company turnover.

- Cold brewed for 18 hours from single-origin Arabica (Colombia and Ethiopia).
- 110 mg of caffeine per can, 2.1 g of sugar per 100 ml (no added sugar).
- Two variants: Original and Vanilla. An Espresso Strong variant is planned for 2026.
- Vegan, lactose-free, organic certified (EU organic label).
- Shelf life of 6 months refrigerated; it needs a chilled shelf.
- Recyclable aluminium can, with the German deposit (Pfand) of 0.25 euros.

In a Danish taste test run by the company in 2024 (300 participants), 52% preferred Nordbrew to the leading canned iced coffee with milk, 18% had no preference and 30% preferred the competitor. Participants liked the low sweetness; 22% found the Original variant too bitter.

Intended shelf price in Germany: 2.49 euros per can (deposit excluded).

## 3. Why Germany, and launch goals

Germany is the largest ready-to-drink (RTD) coffee market in Europe, and oat drinks are the fastest growing plant-based drink category there.

Goals for the German launch, approved by the board in May 2025:

- Reach a 3% value share of the German chilled RTD coffee category within 24 months.
- Be listed in at least 3,500 points of sale in the first year.
- Generate 30% of company turnover from Germany by the end of 2027.

The launch is planned for April 2026, starting with Hamburg, Berlin and the Rhine-Ruhr region.

## 5. Consumer groups

The Getränkepanel 2025 measured interest in "a canned cold brew coffee with oat drink, low sugar, at about 2.50 euros" among German adults aged 18 to 64 (about 52 million people).

| Group | Adults | Share of adults 18-64 | Purchase intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Specialty coffee enthusiasts (home baristas, specialty café regulars) | 0.5 million | 1% | 76% |
| Commuters who buy coffee on the go at least weekly | 11.4 million | 22% | 34% |
| Students | 2.6 million | 5% | 41% |
| Office workers who drink coffee at their desk | 8.9 million | 17% | 27% |

Specialty coffee enthusiasts are by far the most interested group, and they make up 81% of Nordbrew's Instagram community in Denmark. They mostly buy in specialty cafés and online. Commuters mostly buy at petrol stations, kiosks and supermarkets near transport hubs.

## 6. Competitors

- **Emmi Caffè Latte**: the category leader, sold in most supermarkets and petrol stations; milk-based, sweetened.
- **Starbucks chilled cups (Arla)**: strong brand, wide distribution, sweetened milk coffee.
- **Oatly Iced Coffee**: oat-based, smaller distribution, mainly organic shops and supermarkets in large cities.
- **Private labels** of the large discounters: lower prices, entered the category in 2022.

No price comparison has been carried out yet.

## 7. Distribution and marketing

Kaffeekontor Nord is negotiating with one national beverage wholesaler, which supplies supermarkets and petrol stations. A pilot with a train-station kiosk chain (about 400 kiosks) is under discussion. The online shop will ship to Germany from launch.

The marketing budget for the first year is 1.2 million euros.
