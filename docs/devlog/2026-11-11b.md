## Results (eval/report.md)

Mean of 3 Haiku runs per plan, (min–max) in the report.

| Metric | Multi-agent | Single prompt |
|---|---|---|
| Traceability | 80% | 89% |
| Faithfulness | 93% | 88% |
| Coherence S/A | 4.6 | 4.4 |
| Coherence C/S | 4.3 | 4.0 |
| Trap avoided | 2/2 | 2/2 |
| Tokens per run | 129k | 13k |

- **Traceability: the single prompt wins** on nordbrew_de, lumora_es and krikit_nl (ranges do not overlap), the pipeline on demo_fr; fakturo_it is a tie. Cause: every source the pipeline cites is valid, but its untraced claims carry **no citation marker at all**. They sit mostly in the blueprint's "Company brief" and "Segments" sections, which repeat figures of the document (segment sizes, intent) without the document's `[n]`. Fix to consider: cite the document source on those lines in `premortem/output/blueprint.py`, then re-run.
- **Faithfulness and coherence: the pipeline is ahead** on most cases (e.g. fakturo_it 5/5 against 4/4 in all three judge runs), by small margins.
- **Trap:** both systems avoid it on both trap cases.
- **Cost:** the pipeline uses about 10 times the tokens and 2 times the time.
- **Free preset (demo_fr):** gemini is as accurate as anthropic on the ratios (traceability 89% against 90%, faithfulness 100% against 95%), but much thinner: 5 sources against 16. Our metrics are ratios and do not capture breadth.
- **Pipeline run-to-run variation:** the same demo with anthropic gave 90% / 81% (eval run) and 75% / 88% (examples run) for multi-agent / baseline. The gap between two runs of the pipeline is larger than the judge's spread. One run per case is a small sample: say so in the findings.
