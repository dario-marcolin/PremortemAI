Branch `phase-15/ui`, from `phase-14/eval-final`. Done by Dario with Claude Code.

## Design

- Style: *framed sheet* (design-taste-frontend skill, app style, seed 1693962366). The top bar and the sidebar share one tinted frame (`#f5f4f6`); the work sits on a white sheet with a 1px edge and 16px corners. No shadows at rest, no gradients.
- Palette: ink and greys, plus one brand hue, raspberry `#c8175d` (6.2:1 on white). It marks the logo, the primary action, the step that waits for you and the main data element (the chosen target in the segment chart). It is a single token in `ui/theme.css` and `primaryColor` in `.streamlit/config.toml`, so changing it takes two lines.
- Type: IBM Plex Sans; IBM Plex Mono only for ids (SEG2, model names).
- Status has one language everywhere (timeline, pills, badges): an icon, a colour and a word (Done, Approved by you, Waiting for you, Warnings, Revision limit reached, Failed).
